CONCLUSION
=======================================================
v4 (optimized-concise) is the clear winner over v3 (baseline):

- Token savings range from 14% to 29% per call, with the largest gains on
  complex queries (winter-hiking: -29%, three-day-backpacking: -26%).
- Quality scores are virtually identical across all 5 test cases.
- The only minor drop is Relevance on winter-hiking (5→4) and Groundedness
  on day-hike-gear (5→4), both acceptable trade-offs for the cost reduction.
- Prompt tokens dropped from ~165 avg (v3) to ~87 avg (v4) — a 47% reduction
  in system prompt size — without degrading the agent's output quality.

RECOMMENDATION: Merge experiment/optimized-concise to main.
The v4 prompt delivers equivalent guidance with significantly lower API costs.
GPT-4.1-mini experiment (Phase 3) may offer further savings but should be
validated separately before production deployment.

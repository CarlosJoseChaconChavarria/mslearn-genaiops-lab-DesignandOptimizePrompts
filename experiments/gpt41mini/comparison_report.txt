CONCLUSION
==============================================================================================
GPT-4.1-mini with v4 prompt delivers the highest token savings (27-45% vs baseline)
but at a consistent quality drop across all categories (mostly 4/5 vs 5/5).

- Token savings are significant: mini uses 27-45% fewer tokens than baseline.
- Quality is acceptable but noticeably less detailed and nuanced than GPT-4.1.
- For simple, high-volume queries where cost is critical, mini is viable.
- For the primary production deployment, experiment/concise (v4 + GPT-4.1)
  remains the recommended winner — it balances quality and cost better.

RECOMMENDATION: Use GPT-4.1-mini only for simple or high-volume low-stakes queries.
Keep experiment/concise (v4 + GPT-4.1) as the main production deployment.
